# Delete your Share CT data

**Controller Tester** (iOS, iPadOS, macOS) and **Fighter CT** (Android)
Developer: Aaron Lowe

Share CT is the optional global leaderboard. If you never turned it on, the developer holds no data about you and there is nothing to delete.

## In the app (fastest)

Open **Settings › Share CT** and choose **REMOVE MY GLOBAL SCORES**. This erases from the live server your player record, display name, avatar, every score and run, your all-time bests, retry receipts, and any reports that involve you. If the server cannot be reached, the app keeps retrying until the server confirms the erasure.

## Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email [lowekeyflexin@gmail.com](mailto:lowekeyflexin@gmail.com) with the subject "Delete my Share CT data". Include your display name and, if you can see it on the board, the last four characters of your player id. The developer erases the same data the in-app control does and replies when it is done.

## What is kept

Nothing remains on the live leaderboard. The server is backed up nightly and backups are kept for fourteen days, so a copy may persist in a backup until it ages out. Data stored only on your own device (personal bests, CT, cosmetics, settings) is removed when you uninstall the app.
